# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central entry point to OctoAcme's project management guidance. OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative approach with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and regular stakeholder communication. Work moves from initiation and go/no-go, through planning, execution and tracking, release, and retrospective improvement. Teams define success criteria and acceptance criteria, manage risks and dependencies, track work on a project board, validate quality with testing and CI, and use escalation paths and feedback to keep delivery transparent and continuously improve.

- [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md) — validate proposals, align stakeholders, prepare a one-pager, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
- [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) — turn approved work into a prioritized backlog, estimates, Definition of Done, milestones, and risk/dependency plans.
- [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — team rhythm, project-board workflow, pull requests, testing, reporting, and blocker escalation.
- [Risk Management & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — risk lifecycle, stakeholder updates, incident communications, and escalation paths.
- [Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — release requirements, deployment checklist, rollback guidance, and release notes.
- [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — capture learnings, assign action items, and track improvements.
- [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — responsibilities, goals, and communication patterns for developers, product managers, and project managers.
