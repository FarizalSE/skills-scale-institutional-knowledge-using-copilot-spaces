# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Delivery Facilitator helps the delivery team improve flow, follow agreed practices, and remove impediments to sustainable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate ceremonies such as planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove team-level impediments and encourage collaborative problem-solving
- Improve workflow, transparency, and continuous improvement practices
- Help the team maintain sustainable pace and healthy working agreements

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Reduce recurring blockers and process waste
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning

### Typical Communication
- Daily team flow discussions and impediment tracking
- Coordination with the Project Manager on delivery risks and dependencies
- Collaboration with the Product Manager on backlog readiness and meeting effectiveness
- Support and coaching for Developers during execution

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect provides technical direction and ensures that implementation decisions are sound, maintainable, and aligned with project constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and establish design principles
- Identify architecture constraints, technical risks, and integration dependencies
- Support developers with design trade-offs and implementation approaches
- Promote code quality, security, scalability, and operational readiness

### Goals
- Deliver reliable and maintainable technical solutions
- Resolve complex technical decisions and dependencies
- Balance delivery speed with long-term system health

### Typical Communication
- Technical design and architecture reviews with Developers
- Feasibility, trade-off, and sequencing discussions with the Product Manager
- Technical dependency and risk updates to the Project Manager
- Consultation with Security/Compliance and Data/Analytics personas where applicable

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
The QA Lead or Test Manager owns the quality strategy and coordinates the tests, quality gates, and evidence required for release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, coverage expectations, and environments
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Prioritize defects based on customer and delivery risk
- Track quality trends and provide release-readiness assessments

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Increase automated coverage and reduce defect escape risk
- Make quality issues visible and actionable

### Typical Communication
- Work with Developers on testability, automation, and defect resolution
- Align acceptance criteria and quality expectations with the Product Manager
- Report quality risks and release readiness to the Project Manager
- Coordinate security validation with Security/Compliance and operational testing with Support/Operations

---

## UX / Design

### Role Summary
UX and Design professionals define usable, accessible experiences and translate customer and business needs into validated designs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or support user research and usability validation
- Create interaction designs, prototypes, information architecture, and design specifications
- Define usability requirements and contribute to acceptance criteria
- Maintain consistency with established user experience and accessibility standards

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer outcomes
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or unvalidated experiences
- Deliver accessible and consistent user journeys

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with the Product Manager on customer needs and solution priorities
- Pair with Developers on implementation feasibility and design details
- Coordinate usability acceptance with QA/Testing
- Share findings and design decisions with stakeholders

---

## Data / Analytics

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics specialists define measurable outcomes, instrumentation, and reporting that enable evidence-based decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and evaluation methods
- Specify telemetry and measurement requirements
- Build or maintain dashboards and analysis for project signals
- Interpret results and recommend improvements or follow-up actions

### Goals
- Create reliable measurement for outcomes and product usage
- Improve the quality and timeliness of delivery decisions
- Validate whether delivered work achieves expected value

### Typical Communication
- Partner with the Product Manager on success metrics and experiment design
- Coordinate telemetry implementation with Developers
- Provide progress and outcome reporting to the Project Manager and stakeholders
- Supply evidence that helps the Sponsor or Executive Stakeholder make decisions

---

## Security / Compliance

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance professionals ensure that project decisions, implementations, and releases address applicable security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements
- Review high-risk designs, dependencies, and releases
- Coordinate threat modeling, audits, vulnerability remediation, and evidence collection
- Support incident response and operational risk management

### Goals
- Reduce security, privacy, and regulatory risk
- Prevent unresolved risks from reaching production
- Improve auditability and incident readiness

### Typical Communication
- Advise Developers and the Technical Lead on secure design and remediation
- Coordinate validation and evidence with QA/Testing
- Inform the Project Manager of priority risks and required actions
- Escalate material risks and decisions to the Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

---

## Support / Operations

### Role Summary
Support and Operations professionals prepare services for production, represent customer-impact concerns, and sustain reliable operation after release.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare runbooks, monitoring, support procedures, and operational handoffs
- Validate support readiness and production operational needs
- Monitor production health and coordinate issue triage
- Capture recurring customer-impact issues and improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Improve release readiness and operational resilience
- Reduce mean time to detect and resolve production issues
- Ensure a clear transition from delivery to ongoing support

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing on production readiness
- Coordinate deployment and rollback planning with the Project Manager
- Provide stakeholder updates and customer-impact summaries
- Share operational findings with the Technical Lead and Product Manager

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
The Sponsor or Executive Stakeholder provides strategic direction, organizational authority, and decision support for project priorities and business risk.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and approve major scope or investment decisions
- Remove organizational barriers and resolve escalations beyond the team
- Accept or delegate business risk within established authority
- Represent executive stakeholders and provide timely decisions

### Goals
- Keep delivery aligned with organizational strategy and value
- Enable timely resolution of high-impact decisions
- Provide clear ownership and accountability for major outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Receive status, risk, and decision updates from the Project Manager
- Coordinate outcomes and priority trade-offs with the Product Manager
- Review major technical, security, or operational risks with relevant leads
- Engage stakeholders to resolve conflicts or secure approvals
